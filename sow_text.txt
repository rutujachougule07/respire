4. How Attendance and WhatsApp Work Together 
Step 
What happens 
1 
Member checks in on the biometric machine 
2 
The system receives the check-in and marks attendance automatically 
3 
At a time you set, the system lists active members expected that day 
4 
Members on rest day, holiday or freeze are removed from the list 
5 
Members with no check-in are identified as absent 
6 
An approved WhatsApp reminder is sent to absent members who gave consent 
7 
Each absence gets one reminder only; results are logged and visible to the owner and managers 
 
If the biometric machine cannot be connected, managers can enter attendance manually and the WhatsApp reminders will run 
on that data.
